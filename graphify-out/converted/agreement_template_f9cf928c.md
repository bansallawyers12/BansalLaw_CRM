Nature of Service
The services to be performed under this agreement include (but are not limited to):
- Visa eligibility assessment (this includes analysis of the applicable law and policy, and its application to your particular circumstances and goals)
- Providing advice and assistance in relation to the information and documents required to support your application at time of lodgement
- Preparing your application, including any necessary application forms and supporting submissions
- Compiling your application in the required form for lodgement
- Lodging your application with the relevant authority for processing as soon as practicable. Any health examination results and police clearances that become available after lodgement will be added to the application as part of this service
- Monitoring the relevant authority’s processing of your application for up to 18 months after the date of lodgement. This includes advising you of any requests and/or communications received from the relevant authority and notifying you of that authority’s final decision. This service is provided on a complimentary basis only.
Exclusions
The services to be performed under the agreement EXCLUDE:
- Services relating to any associated sponsorship or nomination applications
- Services relating to reviews and appeals
- Services relating to your departure from your country of origin, travel to and entry into Australia
- Professional services required by changes in immigration law or policy after this agreement has been signed by both parties
- Professional services required in response to a court order, subpoena or other legal process for the production of documents or testimony relating to information obtained or prepared under the agreement
- Any services required after the lodgement of your application, unless expressly included above (i.e. any services other than adding health examination results and police clearances received after lodgement, monitoring your application’s progress and notifying you of any requests or communications received). Should you require us to provide further professional services after your application is lodged, another agreement must be entered into and additional fees will apply.


Timeframe
- We estimate that it may take several weeks to lodge your application, with the time taken largely dependent on when you provide the necessary supporting information and documents.
- The timeframe for a decision regarding your application is dependent upon the relevant authority’s stated processing time for this matter. Please note that the actual time that it takes to process your application may also vary depending upon a number of other factors, including the complexity of your case, perceived risk factors and processing priorities.
- Should your matter exceed the stated processing time, we will contact the relevant authority in order to bring your matter to its attention, and facilitate processing. We will not make enquiries regarding the status of your matter if it remains within the stated processing timeframe. Please note that we have no control over processing timeframes, and that we cannot guarantee that your application will be approved or decided by a certain date.
