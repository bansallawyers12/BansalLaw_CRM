Quick reminder

  1. Mail syncs into the CRM mailbox.
  2. System tries to match client/lead.
  3. Assigned = clear match + exactly 1 active matter.
  4. Unassigned = no match, or 2+ active matters.
  5. Use Assign by subject or pick client + matter.
